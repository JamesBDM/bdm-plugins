# BDM Contract Admin

Contract administration under AS4000. Requires **BDM Standards** — the house style and PDF export live there.

- **`bdm-contract-admin-router`** — front door for variations (Form 343 / 342), EOTs (Form 344, cl.34) and the Contract Admin Register. Some sub-skills are not yet recovered; § 2 of that skill sets out the fallback.
- **`bdm-tender-clarification`** — Form 218, live tender. Close date extensions, tenderer RFIs, supplementary drawings.
- **`progress-certificate-update`** — Form 335, certifying a builder's progress claim under cl.37.2.
- **`bdm-site-inspection-report`** — Form 331 from a OneNote "Export to PDF" of a site visit.
- **`bdm-site-and-adhoc-minutes`** — new minutes for site, workshop, kickoff and ad-hoc meetings (Form 232).
- **`meeting-minutes-update`** — rolls PCG and recurring minutes forward with tracked changes.
